# Code of conduct

GerLines is a small volunteer project. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews is expected to follow this code.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, in every language. Many contributors write in their second or third language.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Criticise code, data and decisions with reasons and, where you can, a proposal.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Credit the work of others, including the data providers whose data GerLines uses.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope even when you disagree; you may say why once, clearly.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and comments about a person's identity, background, body or abilities.
- Sexual language or images, and violent or hateful content.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including names behind accounts, without their permission.
- Repeated off-topic posts, spam, and advertising.
- Using the project's spaces to attack railway staff, operators or data providers.

## Reporting

Report a comment, issue or pull request with GitHub's **Report content** menu (the three dots next to it) and
choose to report it to the repository's maintainers. The report is private: only the maintainers see it. Do not
reply to the behaviour in public. Security problems follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What the maintainers do

The maintainers read each report, look at the context and decide in proportion: a reminder, the removal or editing
of the content, locking a conversation, a temporary block, or a permanent block from the repository. They keep the
reporter's identity private. A maintainer who is part of the conflict does not decide on it.

This code applies in every space of the project and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
